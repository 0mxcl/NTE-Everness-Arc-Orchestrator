## 🚀 The Three Pillars of Progression

1.  **Inertia Phase (The Preparation)**:
    - Focus is on **Frictionless Micro-Start** technique. The trainer asks for a "two-minute commitment" and gamifies the act of showing up. This is the hardest phase, so the UI presents a **Zen Garden Interface**—minimal distractions, only the next tiny step.

2.  **Critical Shift (The Breakthrough)**:
    - Once you hit a 70% mastery threshold, the trainer introduces **Chaos Seeds**—deliberately introduced constraints (e.g., "solve the problem using only one hand" or "explain the concept in a haiku"). This forces neural pathway innovation, bypassing the standard "plateau plateau."

3.  **Everness Flow (The Integration)**:
    - Your skill becomes **reflexive and modular**. The trainer now asks you to *teach* the concept to a simulated novice (an AI persona named "NTE-0") to cement the knowledge. This final phase calculates your **Everness Coefficient**, a score that predicts how easily you can re-learn the skill after a long hiatus.
